Inside the sample
The illustration shows the two strands of DNA. When a gene is active, a cell copies information from its DNA into RNA. The studies in this workspace measure RNA abundance to explore how blood cells respond to radiation.
Whole-blood measurements combine signals from several cell types. Their proportions and the way a sample was processed can both affect the expression patterns.
